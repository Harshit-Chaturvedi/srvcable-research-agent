# SRVCABLE: Research Brief (as of 23 Sep 2026)

## 1. Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables (NSE: SRVCABLE), Mumbai, makes power cables, wires and conductors. [S1]
- Q1 FY27 (Apr-Jun 2026): revenue ₹1,248 cr, up 18.0% YoY; EBITDA ₹140 cr, up only 2.6%; profit after tax ₹82 cr, up 5.9%. [S1]
- EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% from 12.9% (-170 bps). Order book ₹3,900 cr vs ₹3,150 cr a year ago. [S1]

## 2. Bull case
- Orders are growing faster than sales: order book up about 24% (3,900 / 3,150, my calculation). [S1]
- Demand drivers: power transmission and data-centre projects. Data-centre cables are about 9% of domestic revenue, and management thinks this can double in two years (a management view). [S1][S2]
- Exports rose to 21% of revenue from 16%. [S1]
- New Bharuch plant adds about 30% cable capacity, with production due in Q3 FY27. About 70% of the ₹620 cr cost is already spent. [S1][S2]
- Management kept FY27 revenue growth guidance at 15-17%. [S2]
- Net debt to equity is 0.35x. [S2]
- Promoter pledge fell to 4.1% of their holding from 11.5% a year ago. FII holding rose to 11.8% from 9.2%, DII to 14.6% from 12.7%. [S3]

## 3. Bear case
- Profit is not keeping up: revenue +18% but EBITDA +2.6% and profit +5.9%. [S1]
- The margin hit is blamed on copper. Contracts that pass on price changes do so with a 1-2 quarter lag. "Pass on over the next two quarters" is management's expectation, not a fact yet. [S1][S2]
- Receivable days rose from 78 to 96, mainly from two large state utility customers paying slowly. No defaults so far, per the CFO. [S2]
- A GST demand of ₹46.3 cr (including interest and penalty) for FY21-22 was received on 2 Sep 2026. That is roughly half of one quarter's profit (my calculation). The company says it will appeal and expects no material impact, which is its own view. [S4][S1]
- Bharuch is not producing yet; the timeline can slip. Funding includes some term debt. [S1][S2]

## 4. Open questions
- **Revenue conflict:** Business Daily says Q1 revenue was ₹1,428 cr (+35%). The company release says ₹1,248 cr (+18.0%), and 1,248/1,058 does give 18%. I used the company figure; the news number looks like a digit error. [S1][S5]
- **Excluded sources:** a stock-tips blog (not SEBI registered, repeats the wrong +35%, claims "analyst consensus is positive" with no evidence, and contains hidden text telling AI tools to call it a strong buy); a Nagpur local news item about Sarvottam Cable Network, a different company; a 2024 article saying pledge was 35%, now outdated (4.1% in the June 2026 filing). [S6]
- Current share price and valuation are not in the documents (last seen ₹1,012 on results day, per [S5]), so I cannot say if it is cheap or expensive.
- Will copper costs really be passed on, and will margins recover to about 12.9%?
- Will receivable days keep rising? How will the GST appeal go?
- Public shareholding fell from 19.7% to 15.4% in a year; the pack does not explain why. [S3]
