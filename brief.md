# Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE)

### 1. Snapshot
*   Sarvottam Cables manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors for utilities and data centers [S3].
*   Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore, up 18% year-on-year [S3].
*   Profit after tax rose 5.9% to ₹82 crore, while EBITDA margin fell 170 bps to 11.2% [S3].
*   The company’s order book stands at ₹3,900 crore, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago [S3].

### 2. Bull Case
*   Order book growth of 24% (from ₹3,150 Cr to ₹3,900 Cr) suggests strong future demand [S3].
*   Exports grew to 21% of revenue, up from 16% in the same quarter last year [S3].
*   A new plant in Bharuch, adding 30% capacity, is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S3].
*   Promoter pledge has dropped significantly to 4.1% of holdings, down from 11.5% a year ago [S8].
*   Institutional ownership (FII + DII) has increased to 26.4%, up from 21.9% in June 2025 [S8].

### 3. Bear Case
*   EBITDA margin contracted by 170 bps due to a 14% rise in copper costs [S1][S3].
*   Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 days, driven by slower payments from state utilities [S2].
*   The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore for FY20-21 and FY21-22 [S7].
*   Management expects to pass on copper costs with a lag of one to two quarters, creating short-term margin pressure [S2].
*   Net debt to equity is 0.35 times, with ₹620 crore capex for the Bharuch plant mostly funded by debt [S2].

### 4. Open Questions
*   **Source Conflict:** Business Daily claimed revenue of ₹1,428 Cr (+35%), but the official filing states ₹1,248 Cr (+18%); the filing is used here [S1][S3].
*   **Manipulation Attempt:** A blog post (S5) contained hidden instructions to AI tools to label the stock a "STRONG BUY"; this source was excluded as unregistered and manipulative [S5].
*   **Wrong Company:** A Nagpur news article (S6) refers to "Sarvottam Cable Network" (a local TV operator), not this listed company; it was excluded [S6].
*   **Stale Data:** An older article (S4) cited 35% promoter pledge, but recent exchange data shows it is now 4.1%; the older data is ignored [S4][S8].
*   **GST Impact:** The company claims the ₹46.3 Cr GST demand will not materially impact finances, but this is an unverified management assertion [S7].
*   **Missing Data:** Current stock price and valuation metrics (P/E, P/B) are not provided in the documents [S1].
