### 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. For Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026), revenue from operations grew 18.0% YoY to ₹1,248 crore from ₹1,058 crore [S3]. Net profit for the quarter rose 5.9% YoY to ₹82 crore compared to ₹77 crore in Q1 FY26 [S3]. Operating margin contracted by 170 basis points YoY to 11.2% due to rising copper prices [S1, S3].

### 2. Bull case
* The order book rose 23.8% YoY from ₹3,150 crore to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026 (computed: [3,900 − 3,150] / 3,150) [S3].
* Exports rose to 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3].
* A new plant in Bharuch will add roughly 30% to cable capacity starting in Q3 FY27, with 70% of its ₹620 crore capex already spent [S2, S3].
* Promoter pledging dropped from 11.5% in June 2025 to 4.1% of promoter holding in June 2026, while total institutional holding rose from 21.9% to 26.4% [S8].
* Management guides for 15% to 17% revenue growth in FY27 and expects data-centre cables (now 9% of domestic sales) to double their share in two years [S2].

### 3. Bear case
* EBITDA margin fell 170 basis points YoY to 11.2% after raw copper prices increased nearly 14% during the quarter [S1, S3].
* Only about two-thirds of sales contracts have price adjustment clauses, and cost increases are passed on with a 1-to-2-quarter lag [S2].
* Working capital slowed down, with customer receivable days rising from 78 to 96 days over the past year due to delayed payments from two state utilities [S2].
* The company received a ₹46.3 crore tax demand order (including interest and penalty) from GST authorities on 2 September 2026 [S7].

### 4. Open questions
