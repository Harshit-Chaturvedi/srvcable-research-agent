### 1. Snapshot

* Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S3].
* For Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026), revenue rose 18.0% year-on-year to ₹1,248 crore from ₹1,058 crore in Q1 FY26 [S3].
* Profit after tax grew 5.9% year-on-year to ₹82 crore, while EBITDA margin declined 170 basis points to 11.2% due to higher copper prices [S3].

---

### 2. Bull case

* The order book expanded by 23.8% year-on-year to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year prior [S3].
* Exports rose as a portion of business, contributing 21% of total revenue in Q1 FY27 compared to 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3].
* A new plant in Bharuch, Gujarat is scheduled to start commercial production in Q3 FY27, adding approximately 30% to total cable manufacturing capacity [S3].
* Promoter pledged shares dropped from 11.5% of promoter holdings in June 2025 to 4.1% in June 2026, while combined institutional ownership (FII and DII) increased from 21.9% to 26.4% [S8].
* Management expects full-year FY27 revenue to grow between 15% and 17% and expects data-centre cable revenue to double over two years [S2].

---

### 3. Bear case

* Operating margins fell by 170 basis points year-on-year to 11.2% because input copper prices rose nearly 14%, and contract price resets lag by one to two quarters [S1, S2, S3].
* Working capital days deteriorated, with customer payment collection (receivable days) rising from 78 days to 96 days over the past year due to delays from two state utilities [S2].
* The company received a tax demand of ₹46.3 crore from the CGST department relating to past input tax credit claims, which represents over half of its quarterly profit [S3, S7].
* The company is funding its ₹620 crore Bharuch plant partially with term debt, resulting in a net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.35 times [S2].
