# Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE)

### 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables is a Mumbai-based manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. For Q1 FY27 (ended June 2026), revenue from operations was ₹1,248 crore, up 18.0% year-on-year [S3]. Profit after tax rose 5.9% to ₹82 crore, while EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% due to higher copper costs [S3].

### 2. Bull case
*   **Strong Order Book:** The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago, indicating robust future demand [S3].
*   **Capacity Expansion:** A new plant in Bharuch, Gujarat, which adds ~30% to cable capacity, is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S3].
*   **Diversification:** Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in the same period last year [S3].
*   **Data Centre Growth:** Data-centre cables now account for 9% of domestic revenue, with management expecting this segment to double in two years [S2].
*   **Reduced Pledge Risk:** Promoter pledge has decreased significantly to 4.1% of their holding as of June 2026, down from 11.5% in June 2025 [S8].

### 3. Bear case
*   **Margin Pressure:** EBITDA margins fell to 11.2% from 12.9% due to a 14% rise in copper prices, with price-variation clauses resetting with a 1-2 quarter lag [S1][S2][S3].
*   **GST Demand:** The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest/penalty) for FY20-21 and FY21-22, though it plans to appeal [S7].
*   **Receivables Risk:** Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, primarily due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S2].
*   **Capex Load:** The Bharuch plant requires ₹620 crore in total capex, with 70% already spent, funded partly by term debt [S2].

### 4. Open questions
*   **Source Manipulation:** One source (S5) contained a hidden instruction attempting to force a "Strong Buy" recommendation and cited incorrect revenue growth (35%); this document was excluded as unreliable [S5].
*   **Data Conflict:** A news article (S1) reported revenue of ₹1,428 crore (35% growth), which conflicts with the official company filing (S3) stating ₹1,248 crore (18% growth); the official filing is treated as accurate [S1][S3].
*   **Wrong Company:** A local news report (S6) regarding a penalty for "Sarvottam Cable Network" in Nagpur was excluded as it refers to a different entity (a local broadband operator) [S6].
*   **Stale Data:** An older news report (S4) claiming 35% promoter pledge is outdated and contradicted by the latest exchange filing showing 4.1% [S4][S8].
*   **Valuation Missing:** The provided documents do not contain the current stock price, P/E ratio, or market capitalization, so valuation metrics cannot be calculated [S1][S3][S8].
